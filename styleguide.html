Design system

Vibe style guide

The building blocks behind the site: colour, type, buttons and their states, navigation, and mobile layouts. Not linked from the public navigation.

Colours are provisional and live as tokens in assets/css/styles.css. Once the official logo is supplied, adjust the brown and sage tokens to match it and the whole site updates.
